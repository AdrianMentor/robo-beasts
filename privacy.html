Privacy Policy: Starfall Defenders

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Starfall Defenders ("the game") is made by Adrian M. This policy explains how the game handles information. In short, the game collects nothing.

What we collect: nothing. - The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. - It has no accounts, logins, chat, ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. - It sends no data to the developer or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device. Your game progress is kept only on your device, in the game's local storage. This includes save data, settings, coins, pets, skins, badges, and daily-chest dates. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data, for the web version) erases it.

Children. The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). This is in line with COPPA and GDPR-K.

Permissions. The app requests no special device permissions.

Third parties. No third-party SDKs that collect data are included. The app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) may collect download and crash statistics under their own privacy policies.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
